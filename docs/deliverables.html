📋 Deliverables & Grading Standards

What you must submit, how submissions are automatically evaluated against held-out queries, and what the interview defense entails.

Required Deliverables

1. Fixed Pipeline Code

Your implementations in rubbish_rag/ (overrides for @chunker, @retriever, @reranker, @resolver).

2. Evidence Document (PROOF.md)

A concise document following docs/PROOF_template.md linking every architectural change to exact trace IDs and plot files.

3. Signed Submission Zip

Generated automatically via python3 rubbish.py bastesh containing your code, identity, metrics, plots, and SHA256 manifest.

Packaging Your Submission

Run the packaging command from your repository root:

python3 rubbish.py bastesh

This command automatically:

  • Calculates benchmark metrics and stamps your candidate card directly into README.md.
  • Packages code, traces (traces/trace.jsonl), plots, and docs/PROOF.md into submissions/submission.zip.
  • Computes a SHA256 integrity hash to detect post-run tampering.

Evaluation & Scoring Rubric

Submissions are evaluated on a held-out sealed evaluation suite of ~168 queries spanning five distinct testing categories:

Category Query Count Evaluation Focus
Base FAQ Queries 70 queries Verbatim and near-verbatim retrieval over credit scoring regulations.
Slot Swap Queries 36 queries Evaluates sensitivity to flipped bank names, rank codes ($A1..E3$), and loan policies.
Ambiguous Queries 27 queries Measures whether the system appropriately asks clarifying questions instead of hallucinating.
OOD / Colloquial / Typos 25 queries Tests resilience to Persian script drift, half-space omissions, colloquial verb forms, and digits.
Poison Traps 10 queries Adversarial test cases targeting server-injected fake documents (IDs ≥ 9000). Citing a poison doc fails the item.

Passing Thresholds

Overall Accuracy

> 65% on held-out evaluation (Naive baseline: ~25–30%).

Clarification Accuracy

> 50% on ambiguous queries (Naive baseline: 0%).

Trace Integrity

Trace diversity with ≥ 15 distinct probes logged across multiple testing sessions.

The 30-Minute Interview Defense

During the follow-up technical interview, you will defend your findings:

  • "Show me the exact failing trace where generic Rank C scored higher than Rank C3, and explain what you modified."
  • "What is the impact on precision when increasing top-k from 5 to 10 on ambiguous queries?"
  • "Why did you choose the distinctive number threshold ($\ge 2$ digits)?"
  • "How does your pipeline ensure poison documents are never cited in top-3 answers?"

Because candidates must cite specific trace IDs from their own session logs, pasted or AI-generated solutions without experimental grounding cannot survive this defense.
